## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-octane, technical-luxe arcade environment tailored for gamified progression, digital asset vaults, and high-value collectibles. It merges the surgical precision of aerospace HUD telemetry with the sensory reward loops of elite gaming terminals. 

### Emotional Persona
- **Sovereign & Prestigious:** Digital ownership feels tangible, fortified, and celebratory through radiant metallic highlights.
- **Electric Precision:** Information architecture is razor-sharp, immediate, and reactive, evoking high-voltage circuitry and fluid modern game interfaces.
- **Dopaminergic Momentum:** Progress meters, dynamic aura rings, and tiered rarity strata trigger continuous accomplishment without visual fatigue.

### Visual Style
A refined synthesis of **Cyber-Luxe & Technical Glassmorphism**:
- Deep, pressurized oceanic navy base structures ground the interface, avoiding pure OLED black to allow subtle luminous backdrops and atmospheric lighting.
- High-refraction gold and energetic volt accents puncture dark surfaces to establish clear visual hierarchy.
- Seamless pill geometries, micro-machined border states, and controlled directional glows create a tactile, luxury vault aesthetic.

## Colors

The palette balances deep naval foundations with radiant spectral accents to communicate collectible tiers and real-time state changes.

### Core Tonal Foundations
- **Base Canvas (`#0A1A2F`):** Deep Navy Tech for foundational root viewports and background layers.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#0E2340`):** Vault card bodies, structural panels, and drawer shells.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#132D50`):** Modals, flyouts, and active elevated states.
- **Borders & Seams (`#1E3A5F` / `#254B78`):** Micro-borders, card seams, and structural dividers.

### Voltage Accents
- **Primary Energy / Gold Volt (`#FFD700`):** Used strictly for high-tier milestone achievements, legendary item frames, primary claim CTAs, and master XP gauges.
- **Secondary Interaction / Volt Blue (`#1E90FF`):** Active interactive triggers, technical telemetry, navigation markers, and micro-switches.
- **Tertiary Mystique / Neon Purple (`#A855F7`):** Epic tier designations, seasonal pass milestones, and auxiliary reward counters.

### Collectible Rarity System
- **Common (`#64748B` - Slate Grey):** Standard utility tokens and common gear. Subdued, low-saturation borders.
- **Rare (`#1E90FF` - Volt Blue):** Core digital collectibles. Crisp neon line accents.
- **Epic (`#A855F7` - Neon Purple):** High-tier unlockables. Saturated micro-auras and radial light rings.
- **Legendary (`#FFD700` - Radiant Gold):** Supreme assets. Outer ambient gold blooms and dual-tone gradients.

### Accessibility Protocol
All interface labels placed on navy surfaces maintain a contrast ratio exceeding 4.5:1 (WCAG AA). White and metallic texts leverage `#F8FAFC` and `#E2E8F0` for readability across high-luminance states.

## Typography

The typography architecture uses structured structural contrast: dynamic geometric impact in the headings and telemetry labels paired with high-efficiency scanning for body and numbers.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Headlines, Telemetry, and Badges):** Provides structural solidity with curved geometric finishes. Used for all high-level display values, asset naming, and uppercase badges.
- **Hanken Grotesk (Body & Sub-text):** Neutral, modern neo-grotesque clarity built for reading micro-copy, terms, and technical collectible specs without visual friction.
- **Tabular Numerics:** All progress indicators, balance counters, timers, and stat matrices must enforce tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent layout shifts during animated counting sequences.
- **Micro-Labels (`label-caps`):** Technical vault sub-headers, tier chips, and system status indicators are rendered in strict uppercase with tracked spacing (`0.12em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model runs on a modular 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to support dense telemetry alongside showcase grid galleries.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Bottom sheets replace standard dropdown panels; interactive pills span full viewport width.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin-tablet: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, max-width container locked to `1440px`, centered with `margin: 3rem`.

### Spacing Principles
- **Vault Density Rule:** Component internal padding uses tight multiples of 4px (`space-sm`, `space-md`) to emulate condensed tactical gear, while separation between modular cards relies on expansive gaps (`space-lg`, `space-xl`) to establish distinct visual framing.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Page header elements and section divides enforce `space-xl` separation to give legendary showcase pieces immediate prominence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is built through structured tonal layers augmented by glassmorphism and targeted neon glows rather than diffuse muddy dropshadows.

### Structural Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Viewport Ground):** Solid `#0A1A2F` underlay with an ultra-subtle directional gradient radial bleed centered behind hero artifacts.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Vault Enclosures):** `#0E2340` at 90% opacity paired with a `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a continuous 1px perimeter border rendered in `#1E3A5F`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Modals & Dropdown Matrices):** `#132D50` with a 1px perimeter in `#254B78` and a deep floor shadow (`0 20px 40px -15px rgba(2, 6, 18, 0.7)`).

### Atmospheric Glow & Rarity Shadows
Elevated components and rarity cards emit localized chromatic glows:
- **Legendary Bloom:** `0 0 25px -4px rgba(255, 215, 0, 0.35)` combined with a razor-thin internal edge highlight (`inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`).
- **Volt Pulse:** `0 0 20px -3px rgba(30, 144, 255, 0.3)`.
- **Epic Radiance:** `0 0 20px -3px rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.3)`.
- **Common Utility:** Zero shadow; uses a clean 1px hairline border in `#1E3A5F`.

## Shapes

The design system implements a pill-shaped aesthetic (`roundedness: 3`).

### Geometry Guidelines
- **Fully Rounded Pills (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Primary interactive CTA buttons, status badges, telemetry pills, category filters, and avatar frames.
- **Large Component Enclosures (`rounded-xl` / 3rem):** Main collectible showcase cards, vault display hubs, and modal windows.
- **Micro-Elements (`rounded-lg` / 2rem):** Input fields, segmented switches, and inline progress track rails.
- **Contour Insets:** Border widths are constrained to 1px or 2px to balance soft, pill-shaped edges with sharp, technical borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Reward / Action Button:** Full pill shape (`rounded-full`). Solid Gold Volt background (`#FFD700`) with high-contrast `#0A1A2F` text in `Plus Jakarta Sans` Bold. Interactive hover triggers a scaled luminous aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255, 215, 0, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary Volt Action:** Full pill shape. Deep surface `#0E2340` with a 1.5px border of `#1E90FF` and electric blue text. Hover fills background to 15% blue tint.
- **Ghost Utility:** Borderless pill with `#64748B` typography, transitioning to clean white `#F8FAFC` on focus/hover.

### Chips & Rarity Badges
- Compact pill forms (`rounded-full`) with uppercase tracking (`label-caps`).
- Constructed with a 15% opacity tint of the rarity color fill, surrounded by a 1px crisp solid border in that rarity color (e.g., Epic uses 15% `#A855F7` background with a solid `#A855F7` border).
- Include a leading 6px circular LED indicator dot that pulses softly for active states.

### Collectible Vault Cards
- Structural base in `#0E2340` with `rounded-xl` boundaries and 1px border (`#1E3A5F`).
- **Dynamic Tier Frame:** For Rare, Epic, and Legendary cards, the border shifts to the respective tier color accompanied by a top edge gradient highlight.
- Interior layout separates the asset render canvas, title block, rarity tag, and bottom telemetry footer with a `#1E3A5F` divider line.

### Progress Rings & Gauges
- **SVG Circular Micro-Gauges:** Vector-based circular rings displaying current level or quest progress. Track background is rendered in `#1E3A5F` with the active stroke rendered in `#FFD700` or `#1E90FF` using `stroke-linecap: round`.
- **Linear Progress Bars:** Pill-shaped tracks (`rounded-full`) with a recessed `#0A1A2F` groove and an illuminated volt-fill bar featuring an animated shimmer highlight.

### Input Fields
- Enclosed pill shapes (`rounded-lg` / 2rem) with `#0A1A2F` background and 1px `#1E3A5F` border.
- Placeholder text in `#64748B`. Focus state transitions border to `#1E90FF` with a subtle outer volt glow (`0 0 12px rgba(30, 144, 255, 0.25)`).

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Checkboxes:** Smooth squircle-pills with 1.5px `#1E3A5F` border. Checked state transitions instantly to `#FFD700` background with dark navy checkmark icon.
- **Segmented Control Tabs:** Outer pill capsule housing an animated sliding pill indicator that docks behind the active item with `#132D50` surface fill and a 1px border.
